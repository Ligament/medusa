# {metadata.title}

In this guide, you'll learn how to create a category bundle that customers purchase as a subscription with recurring delivery rounds. This guide builds on the [Create Category Bundle Product](../page.mdx) guide, which explains how category bundles and their selection groups work.

## Overview

A subscription bundle works like a one-time category bundle, with a delivery and renewal plan on top:

- Each payment grants the customer a number of delivery rounds, set in **Rounds per purchase**.
- The customer picks the bundle's products for every delivery round, following the same selection groups.
- When the remaining rounds run low, the subscription renews. Depending on the **Payment mode**, Medusa either charges the customer automatically or notifies them to pay manually.
- The price of the bundle's linked product is the amount charged per payment, covering all the rounds in **Rounds per purchase**.

---

## Create a Subscription Bundle

To create a subscription bundle:

1. Go to **Category Bundles** from the sidebar.
2. Click the **Create Bundle** button at the top right. This opens the "Create category bundle" form.
3. Enter a **Bundle name**. Medusa uses this name for the linked product it creates automatically.
4. Keep the **Subscription bundle** toggle enabled. It's enabled by default, and the **Subscription plan** panel shows the delivery and renewal settings.

![Create category bundle form with the Subscription plan panel](/user-guide/images/category-bundles/create-form-subscription.jpg)

5. Configure the bundle's selection groups, as explained in the [Configure Bundle Groups](../page.mdx#configure-bundle-groups) section of the category bundle guide.
6. Configure the delivery and renewal rules, as explained in the [Configure the Subscription Plan](#configure-the-subscription-plan) section.
7. Once you're done, click the **Create bundle** button.

<Note title="Warning" type="warning">

Choose the bundle type before you create the bundle. You can't switch a bundle between one-time and subscription after creation.

</Note>

### Delivery Capacity

- **Rounds per purchase**: The number of delivery rounds added after each successful charge.
- **Maximum delivery days per week**: The number of delivery days the customer can select per week. For example, set it to 1 to allow one delivery per week.
- **Selection cutoff days**: The number of days before a delivery when the customer's selections lock.

### Renewal and Payment

- **Renew when rounds remain**: The renewal starts when this number of delivery rounds remain.
- **Payment mode**: Choose **Auto charge** to charge the customer automatically on renewal, or **Notify only** to notify the customer to pay manually.
- **Subscription expiration**: The subscription's lifetime in days. Leave it blank to keep the subscription active until it's cancelled.

## Set the Price and Publish

Set the price on the bundle's linked product and publish it, as explained in the [Set the Bundle Price and Publish](../page.mdx#set-the-bundle-price-and-publish) section of the category bundle guide.

For a subscription bundle, the price is the amount charged per payment, which covers the delivery rounds set in **Rounds per purchase**. For example, if the bundle has four rounds per purchase and the price is 2,000 THB, the customer pays 2,000 THB for every four delivery rounds.

## Manage Subscriptions

### Bundle Details Page

The bundle's details page in **Category Bundles** shows the number of active subscriptions that reference the bundle, and a configuration record that captures the bundle's settings so that changes don't affect existing subscriptions.

<Note title="Warning" type="warning">

Editing a bundle that has active subscriptions affects the delivery rounds of existing subscribers. Check the subscription count on the details page before you change delivery or renewal rules.

</Note>

### Subscriptions Section

Go to **Subscriptions** from the sidebar to manage the operational side of subscription bundles. The section shows:

- **Orders to ship**: Subscription orders due in the next seven days that haven't shipped.
- **Upcoming subscription deliveries**: A calendar of the deliveries scheduled for the next seven days.
- Counters of active, expiring, and expired subscriptions.
- The full list of subscriptions with their remaining rounds, next delivery, expiration, and status.

You can also export the list with **Export CSV**, or create a subscription on a customer's behalf with **Create Subscription**.
